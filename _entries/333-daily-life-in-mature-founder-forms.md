Once the dramatic conversion is over, Riven and Blade still have to live in bodies with six arms, movable face plates, active nanites, floating structures, and unusually dense maintenance systems. Mundane consequences are what make the founder forms feel real instead of existing only for transformation scenes.

- Clothing needs three shoulder levels and adaptive openings.

- Furniture has to account for tails, lower arms, and orbital plates.

- Sleeping positions change around six arms and floating components.

- Grooming and plate maintenance become longer but partly automated by nanites.

- Public spaces can be awkward even when nothing is medically wrong.

### **DEEPER CANON**

A normal doorway is not necessarily too narrow, but walking through it while carrying something in four hands and gesturing with the other two is a coordination problem. Chairs with rigid side walls can trap lower arms. Backpacks designed for two shoulders may block an entire arm pair. Seatbelts need routes that do not cut across active BlackGlass seams.

The founders eventually develop unconscious habits: folding one pair behind or across the torso, parking orbital plates closer in crowds, narrowing Fractureface gaps in dusty environments, and arranging tails automatically before sitting.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Vitrites handle thousands of maintenance tasks during ordinary life: cleaning fine debris from plate seams, checking floating bridge strength, correcting fur caught in interfaces, balancing local temperature, and testing duplicated shoulder joints for early strain.

This automation is why mature forms can feel natural instead of requiring constant conscious micromanagement.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Convenience should not erase accessibility needs. A building designed only for standard humanoid bodies can still be annoying or unsafe. The founders can adapt around bad design, but the setting should not use their mutation ability as an excuse to never accommodate them.

Similarly, six arms do not mean they must always multitask. Sometimes four hands are just resting.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Write at least one slice-of-life scene where a founder form matters without danger.

- Design clothing as actual six-arm clothing rather than a two-arm shirt with random holes.

- Let environmental accessibility become part of worldbuilding.
