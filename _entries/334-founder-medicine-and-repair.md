Treating Riven or Blade requires understanding that their BlackGlass systems are active participants in medicine. A clinician is not repairing an inert machine or an ordinary animal body; they are negotiating with a self-modifying anatomy map that may already be halfway through its own solution.

- Medical scans compare active map, archive, and current physical state.

- Nanites should usually be coordinated with rather than blindly suppressed.

- Origin cores can lock authoring during surgery.

- Floating gaps and decoupling seams are not automatically injuries.

- Ancestral and later-authored anatomy are both treated as real body parts.

### **DEEPER CANON**

A scanner must distinguish a planned open seam from a tear, an echo limb from a permanent limb, and a temporary support plate from uncontrolled growth. That is why founder medical records include body-map state in addition to ordinary imaging.

Riven's permanent floating lower anatomy is especially easy for an uninformed system to misclassify as catastrophic dismemberment. Blade's selective decoupling can create the same problem temporarily. Species-aware medicine recognizes the nanite bridge and checks its stability instead of panicking at the empty space.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

During surgery, Vitrites can retract from a working area, hold microstructures stable, or provide internal illumination and sensor data. If the medical team fights them without a reason, the body may interpret the procedure as damage and start closing the very area the clinician needs open.

Origin Glasshearts can create a temporary maintenance freeze around noncritical authoring so the body does not decide to redesign a healing joint halfway through treatment.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Founder regeneration is not a reset button. Rebuilding a major limb consumes enormous material, and the regenerated structure can still need rehabilitation. Neural maps may be accurate while muscle strength, balance, and learned fine control lag behind.

Medical consent remains important even when the body can heal itself. The existence of nanites does not make experimentation acceptable.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Develop species-aware diagnostic symbols later.

- Keep rehabilitation relevant after major reconstruction.

- Use medical misunderstanding as a worldbuilding problem without making competent care impossible.

### Human Notes & Practical Detail
**Founder Medicine and Repair** is one of those details that can look simple on a reference sheet and turn into a dozen small decisions the moment the character starts moving.

The useful questions here are **Generation-Zero core architecture, founder-only body-map authoring, owner canon, fatigue limits, and the difference between precedent and a public trait list**. That is the technical side, but it should show up in ordinary life instead of only in diagrams. With **Founder Medicine and Repair**, practical details can include ordinary social interaction, sleep, clothing, and movement. A character who has had the feature for years usually handles those things without thinking; somebody who acquired it recently may still misjudge space, overcorrect, or consciously plan movements that will eventually become automatic.

Decide what **Founder Medicine and Repair** feels like from the inside. It might register as ordinary proprioception, pressure, a quiet digital position sense, background diagnostics, or almost nothing until something goes wrong. Useful warning signs for this feature include reduced precision, extra heat or energy use, and slower response. Those signs do not need to mean catastrophic failure. Noxivitre systems usually degrade in layers, so reduced precision or a safer fallback state often appears before anything truly dangerous happens.

For artists and writers, give **Founder Medicine and Repair** at least one repeatable visual or behavioral tell. Possibilities include a more compact resting state, subtle BlackGlass seams, and visible nanite traffic under load. Keep the tell consistent enough that it feels like the same body from scene to scene. Also test the feature somewhere inconvenient but mundane: a cramped vehicle, an unfamiliar bed, a clinic designed for fixed anatomy, or a crowded hallway. If the design still has a believable answer there—perhaps through clothing, maintenance, and movement—the rule is doing real work instead of existing only for spectacle.

If a character breaks the normal version of **Founder Medicine and Repair**, write down what is compensating for the exception and what tradeoff appears somewhere else. Founder capability still has costs. Riven and Blade can author pathways that later Noxivitres normally cannot, but fatigue, risk, preference, and owner canon still matter. Rarity should still be judged from the whole build. A visually extreme version can remain inside the open tiers, including Mythic, when it does not use creator-reserved founder authority. Unobtanium is not a reward for having the loudest silhouette.
