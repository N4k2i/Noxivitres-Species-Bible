Riven and Blade are Unobtanium because their mature Generation-Zero bodies contain founder-class systems that sit outside the normal open rarity ladder. The visible mutations are not the lock. The lock is what their cores can author, stabilize, and remember.

- Both possess Origin Glasshearts rather than ordinary later-generation Glasshearts.

- Both can establish new stable personal body-map branches through founder-class authoring.

- Both retain unusually deep mutation memory across major structural rewrites.

- Both can run multiple incompatible-looking body states without losing identity continuity.

- Their founder functions are owner-controlled canon and do not become public traits by imitation.

### **DEEPER CANON**

A Mythic Noxivitre can perform enormous transformations using body-map branches that its biology already knows, develops through normal Glassshifts, inherits from its origin, or learns through stable adaptation. Riven and Blade can go one layer deeper: their Origin Glasshearts can create a new branch, test it against the entire body, and if it remains coherent, store it as something the body now recognizes as itself.

That does not mean they can instantly become anything imaginable. The new branch still needs material, neural mapping, structural support, energy, nanite labor, and a shape that the person's body can physically maintain. Founder authoring is closer to writing new anatomy into a living operating system than to reality warping.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

During authoring, BlackGlass becomes visibly active around the structures being rewritten. Nanite density increases, local plates may separate, and temporary scaffold anatomy can appear before permanent tissue or machinery finishes forming. The Origin Glassheart compares the experimental branch against identity anchors so a successful mutation extends the person rather than replacing them.

The founders' nanites also remember failed attempts more precisely than ordinary strains. A failed branch can be blacklisted, revised, or partially reused later instead of being rediscovered from zero. This is one reason their later mutations become more controlled even as the bodies become more complex.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Unobtanium does not mean effortless. A new branch can leave the founder exhausted, overheated, hungry, nanite-depleted, temporarily clumsy, or stuck maintaining scaffold structures longer than expected. The body can refuse an authoring attempt if too many major systems are already damaged.

The rarity also does not make them socially superior. Someone can dislike them, outrank them professionally, beat them at a game, outsmart them, or simply not care. Unobtanium is a species-classification and permission term, not a personality replacement.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Treat authoring as a process with stages, not a magic instant.

- Let failed or abandoned branches leave useful mutation memory.

- Keep public Mythic anatomy broad enough that founder rarity never depends on hoarding ordinary cool traits.
