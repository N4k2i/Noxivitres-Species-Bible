Riven’s floating legs and tail become substantially more Noxivitre-like after conversion because BlackGlass stops treating the empty space as a weird exception and starts treating the separation itself as an active anatomical system.

- Legs and tail remain physically separated from the torso.

- Nanite bridges carry sensation, motor intent, power, and repair instructions across the gaps.

- Gap width can change within safe mapped limits.

- The components can rotate or reposition more freely than before conversion.

- Emergency anchor behavior prevents casual loss of a limb when fields are disrupted.

### **DEEPER CANON**

Before conversion, the simple fact that the limbs floated was the unusual trait. After conversion, the interesting part is how alive the gap becomes. BlackGlass can thicken the bridge during a jump, narrow it while Riven is exhausted, reroute signal around interference, or temporarily grow support shards through the empty space when a limb needs more physical stability.

The tail gains especially expressive freedom. It can hover slightly farther from the body when alert, pull close when defensive, or shift its base position enough to avoid furniture and the lower pair of arms. None of this requires the tail to become a free-flying drone; it remains a body part with a mapped home region.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Vitrites constantly calculate load and momentum. A floating leg landing from a jump creates force that must still be transferred into the rest of the body. The bridge does that through field coupling, temporary BlackGlass reinforcement, and distributed correction rather than pretending gravity stopped applying.

If nanite density drops, the body reduces the maximum safe separation and movement range before it gives up basic locomotion. This is why resource exhaustion changes Riven's posture before it necessarily makes the legs fail.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

The legs cannot simply be left in another building while Riven walks away on invisible force. Safe separation has a range, and high-force movement requires much tighter control than a relaxed pose. Severe interference can force the system into an emergency close-lock where floating segments pull nearer to their mapped attachment zones.

Riven can still conceal the gaps visually in clothing or posture, but the mature system may betray itself through particle movement or the way fabric crosses empty space.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Define safe resting, active, and emergency gap ranges later if exact measurements are needed.

- Let the tail use floating freedom for body language without making it an independent character.

- Keep force transfer physically legible during running, jumping, and impacts.
