Blade’s Unobtanium systems are constrained by material, energy, coordination, and the fact that precision itself takes processing. His Mirror Lattice is extremely good at keeping complex anatomy coherent, but it cannot make complexity free.

- Duplication consumes real mass and stored material.

- Decoupling has range and force limits.

- Mirror diagnostics can be overwhelmed by too many changing structures.

- The Origin Glassheart can suspend authoring to protect permanent anatomy.

- Owner-defined identity traits are protected against unwanted auto-correction.

### **DEEPER CANON**

Blade's most characteristic failure mode is not random chaos but increasing simplification. When the system is overloaded, echo structures vanish, tail segments close, orbital plates settle into fewer positions, crown shards pull close, and the Fractureface reduces unnecessary separation. The body becomes easier to calculate before it becomes nonfunctional.

That behavior reflects the Mirror Lattice's priorities: preserve a known stable configuration first, then restore complexity when resources return.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Vitrites can temporarily lower diagnostic frequency in noncritical systems to concentrate on an injury. Blade may feel this as slightly delayed plate movement, less precise crown-shard positioning, or a need to consciously manage limbs that normally feel automatic.

The core is especially cautious about making permanent changes while data is unreliable. Severe sensory interference can lock out authoring until the environment improves.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Blade cannot simply duplicate another person's rare trait into himself because he saw it. His core authors from his own body map and owner-approved mutation logic.

Likewise, perfect structural symmetry does not guarantee perfect skill. A duplicated hand has the anatomy to perform a task, but Blade still has to learn how to use it.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Use simplification as Blade’s readable low-resource state.

- Keep external copying out of the Mirror Lattice unless @altblade explicitly adds it later.

- Separate anatomical precision from learned skill.
