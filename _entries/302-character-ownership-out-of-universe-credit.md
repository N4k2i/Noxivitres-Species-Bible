The NOXIVITRES species is an open species through Mythic, but Riven and Blade are individually owned characters. Species permissions and character ownership are separate on purpose, and the bible should never blur them.

- Riven Solace is owned by @n4k2 on Discord.

- Blade is owned by @altblade on Discord.

- The owner credit is meta information, not an in-universe label characters walk around saying.

- Sharing this bible does not grant ownership of either founder.

- Unobtanium permission does not automatically include permission to use Riven or Blade.

### **DEEPER CANON**

A person can make a six-armed Noxivitre, a white Noxivitre, a pink Noxivitre, a floating-legged Noxivitre, or even a creator-approved Unobtanium Noxivitre without that character becoming Riven or Blade. Character identity comes from the complete design, personality, history, relationships, name, and owner-controlled canon, not from one anatomical trait.

If the bible is excerpted into a website, reference sheet, wiki, or species guide, the founder section should preserve these credits somewhere visible. This is especially important because the founders also serve as anatomy examples; an example image or profile should not be mistaken for a free base or public adoptable.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

BlackGlass has no concept of Discord ownership; this section exists outside the fiction. In-universe, the equivalent issue is personhood and bodily autonomy: being historically important does not make either founder public property, a research specimen people can claim, or a biological resource other characters are entitled to sample.

That parallel is useful because the species already deals heavily with mutable bodies. The same setting that says a body can change dramatically should be equally clear that the person still owns their own body and identity.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

When collaborators add founder lore, ownership means major permanent changes should be checked against the relevant owner instead of being silently treated as communal species canon. Shared species mechanics can be proposed broadly; Riven-specific and Blade-specific facts stay attached to their owners.

A later owner-approved retcon can override details in this section, but it should be written as a clear revision rather than leaving two contradictory founder profiles floating around.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Credit both character owners whenever both founders are profiled together.

- Separate species trait permissions from character-use permissions.

- Keep meta ownership text outside in-universe dialogue unless a fourth-wall project intentionally needs it.

### Human Notes & Practical Detail
The best way to understand **Character Ownership & Out-of-Universe Credit** is to imagine the character dealing with it on a boring Tuesday, not only during a transformation scene.

The useful questions here are **Generation-Zero core architecture, founder-only body-map authoring, owner canon, fatigue limits, and the difference between precedent and a public trait list**. That is the technical side, but it should show up in ordinary life instead of only in diagrams. With **Character Ownership & Out-of-Universe Credit**, practical details can include ordinary social interaction, movement, clothing, and privacy. A character who has had the feature for years usually handles those things without thinking; somebody who acquired it recently may still misjudge space, overcorrect, or consciously plan movements that will eventually become automatic.

Decide what **Character Ownership & Out-of-Universe Credit** feels like from the inside. It might register as ordinary proprioception, pressure, a quiet digital position sense, background diagnostics, or almost nothing until something goes wrong. Useful warning signs for this feature include temporary fallback behavior, extra heat or energy use, and slower response. Those signs do not need to mean catastrophic failure. Noxivitre systems usually degrade in layers, so reduced precision or a safer fallback state often appears before anything truly dangerous happens.

For artists and writers, give **Character Ownership & Out-of-Universe Credit** at least one repeatable visual or behavioral tell. Possibilities include visible nanite traffic under load, subtle BlackGlass seams, and a more compact resting state. Keep the tell consistent enough that it feels like the same body from scene to scene. Also test the feature somewhere inconvenient but mundane: a cramped vehicle, an unfamiliar bed, a clinic designed for fixed anatomy, or a crowded hallway. If the design still has a believable answer there—perhaps through sleep, privacy, and clothing—the rule is doing real work instead of existing only for spectacle.

If a character breaks the normal version of **Character Ownership & Out-of-Universe Credit**, write down what is compensating for the exception and what tradeoff appears somewhere else. Founder capability still has costs. Riven and Blade can author pathways that later Noxivitres normally cannot, but fatigue, risk, preference, and owner canon still matter. Rarity should still be judged from the whole build. A visually extreme version can remain inside the open tiers, including Mythic, when it does not use creator-reserved founder authority. Unobtanium is not a reward for having the loudest silhouette.

#### Character-facing note

**Character Ownership & Out-of-Universe Credit** can shape habits, accessibility needs, confidence, embarrassment, fashion, or body language, but it should not replace the character's personality. Noxivitres can have extremely different bodies without those bodies deciding morality, intelligence, social importance, or fighting ability. That separation is important to the open-species side of the canon.
