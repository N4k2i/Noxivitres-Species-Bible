Mythic is the highest open rarity and should remain capable of absurd, anatomy-breaking designs. Founder-class Unobtanium is a different category of permission and core behavior, not a bigger pile of visible traits.

- Mythic can have more limbs than Riven or Blade.

- Mythic can be larger, stranger, more transformed, or visually louder.

- Mythic can have powerful Glassshifts and multiple stable forms.

- Unobtanium is defined by creator-approved canon systems such as Origin-class authoring.

- Visual extremity alone never forces a character into Unobtanium.

### **DEEPER CANON**

A twelve-armed Mythic with three tails, two wing pairs, a huge Fractureface, and multiple floating torso segments can still be completely open if its Glassheart operates through normal Noxivitre mutation pathways. Meanwhile, a visually restrained Unobtanium might look almost ordinary until a scanner reveals a core capable of authoring new body-map branches.

This separation is intentional. It means people using the open species do not have to make their designs boring to respect creator-locked lore.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

The internal difference appears most clearly during novel adaptation. A Mythic body chooses among known, inherited, learned, or naturally developed branches. An Origin-class Unobtanium core can draft a branch that did not previously exist for that individual, test it, and potentially commit it as new stable anatomy.

Both processes still rely on BlackGlass and nanites, so the categories feel related rather than like two unrelated power systems.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Unobtanium should not automatically win fights, heal faster in every circumstance, or possess every Mythic trait. A specialized Mythic can outperform a founder in a specific task if its body is built for that task.

The lock should stay narrow enough that open creators can explore the species instead of constantly discovering that every interesting concept was reserved.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- When classifying a design, ask what the core does before counting limbs.

- Keep Mythic visually unrestricted.

- Reserve Unobtanium for explicit creator-approved canon mechanics, not aesthetic flexing.

### Human Notes & Practical Detail
A good version of **Mythic Versus Founder-Class Unobtanium** feels specific to the person wearing it. The system can be technically complicated without making the character talk like a manual.

The useful questions here are **Generation-Zero core architecture, founder-only body-map authoring, owner canon, fatigue limits, and the difference between precedent and a public trait list**. That is the technical side, but it should show up in ordinary life instead of only in diagrams. With **Mythic Versus Founder-Class Unobtanium**, practical details can include privacy, maintenance, ordinary social interaction, and clothing. A character who has had the feature for years usually handles those things without thinking; somebody who acquired it recently may still misjudge space, overcorrect, or consciously plan movements that will eventually become automatic.

Decide what **Mythic Versus Founder-Class Unobtanium** feels like from the inside. It might register as ordinary proprioception, pressure, a quiet digital position sense, background diagnostics, or almost nothing until something goes wrong. Useful warning signs for this feature include temporary fallback behavior, extra heat or energy use, and reduced precision. Those signs do not need to mean catastrophic failure. Noxivitre systems usually degrade in layers, so reduced precision or a safer fallback state often appears before anything truly dangerous happens.

For artists and writers, give **Mythic Versus Founder-Class Unobtanium** at least one repeatable visual or behavioral tell. Possibilities include visible nanite traffic under load, subtle BlackGlass seams, and a more compact resting state. Keep the tell consistent enough that it feels like the same body from scene to scene. Also test the feature somewhere inconvenient but mundane: a cramped vehicle, an unfamiliar bed, a clinic designed for fixed anatomy, or a crowded hallway. If the design still has a believable answer there—perhaps through sleep, maintenance, and ordinary social interaction—the rule is doing real work instead of existing only for spectacle.

If a character breaks the normal version of **Mythic Versus Founder-Class Unobtanium**, write down what is compensating for the exception and what tradeoff appears somewhere else. Founder capability still has costs. Riven and Blade can author pathways that later Noxivitres normally cannot, but fatigue, risk, preference, and owner canon still matter. Rarity should still be judged from the whole build. A visually extreme version can remain inside the open tiers, including Mythic, when it does not use creator-reserved founder authority. Unobtanium is not a reward for having the loudest silhouette.

#### Character-facing note

**Mythic Versus Founder-Class Unobtanium** can shape habits, accessibility needs, confidence, embarrassment, fashion, or body language, but it should not replace the character's personality. Noxivitres can have extremely different bodies without those bodies deciding morality, intelligence, social importance, or fighting ability. That separation is important to the open-species side of the canon.
