Blade develops floating BlackGlass plates around the shoulders, flanks, and back that act as adaptive armor, six-arm clearance structures, and visual markers of Noxivitre maturity. His plate system tends to favor cleaner repeated shapes and paired movement.

- Plates occupy mapped orbital positions rather than permanent bolts.

- Paired plates often mirror one another but can break symmetry when needed.

- They move automatically around all six arm arcs.

- They can close over vulnerable seams during repair.

- They retain Blade’s personal palette and design language.

### **DEEPER CANON**

Blade's plates can make his silhouette look more constructed than Riven's while still being grown anatomy. Repeated shapes may line up into clean rows when resting and then fan apart when several arms move at once. The effect should feel intentional rather than randomly glitchy.

Because the plates are part of the body, they can carry sensation at their inner surfaces and positional awareness throughout. Blade knows when a plate is being pushed out of place even if he cannot see it.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

The Mirror Lattice predicts common arm paths and moves plates before collision happens. This is an example of mature nanite behavior becoming mundane: Blade does not need to think 'move left shoulder plate three centimeters' every time he reaches across his chest.

When damaged, a plate can be held in a simplified position while Vitrites rebuild the edge, then return to its normal orbit once the core confirms its balance.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Orbital plates are not infinite shields. They cover limited zones and can be forced aside by enough impact. Packing them too tightly reduces ventilation, joint freedom, and nanite traffic.

As with crown shards, a plate that starts drifting asymmetrically can signal fatigue before Blade verbally acknowledges it.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Use paired plate motion as Blade-specific body language.

- Let the plates visibly solve arm-clearance problems.

- Avoid making the system a permanent perfect shield.
