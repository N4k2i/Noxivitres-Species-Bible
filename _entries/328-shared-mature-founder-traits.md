Riven and Blade grow into visibly different Noxivitres, but several mature systems are shared because they descend from the same Generation-Zero BlackGlass breakthrough. These shared traits are strong examples of species identity, not automatically Unobtanium-only visuals.

- Six permanent arms.

- True Fractureface architecture.

- Origin Glassheart cores.

- Vitrite nanites integrated as a normal organ system.

- Orbital or floating BlackGlass structures.

- Permanent body maps capable of supporting multiple stable configurations.

### **DEEPER CANON**

The shared systems are useful because they establish what survived two different starting anatomies. If both founders independently converge on fracture-based facial architecture, distributed BlackGlass channels, six-arm torso integration, and floating support structures, those concepts become strong candidates for the species' visual language even though later Noxivitres may express them differently.

At the same time, convergence should not erase difference. Riven's sensory crown, permanent floating lower body, and adaptive spatial lattice are not Blade's crown shards, selective decoupling, and mirror lattice.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Their Vitrites can recognize the other founder's swarm as related without confusing ownership. This lets the systems exchange limited stabilization data or synchronize environmental readings when they are close, but every repair decision still routes through the body it belongs to.

Shared founder protocols later become the conceptual basis for safe Noxivitre-to-Noxivitre medical interfaces.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

A later Common or Uncommon Noxivitre can still have a Fractureface. A Rare can have orbital plates. A Mythic can have six arms and floating legs. The shared founder list is not a restricted-trait shopping list.

The Unobtanium lock applies to the founder-class Origin implementation and owner-controlled canon, not to the broad aesthetics the species is built around.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Use founder convergence to define species identity without forcing clones.

- Keep Riven/Blade differences visible even when their systems share a name.

- Allow open-tier characters to reinterpret shared visuals with simpler core mechanics.
