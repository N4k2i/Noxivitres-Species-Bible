Functional Wings is a morphology option rather than a mandatory species trait. It should still look BlackGlass-built: the joints, attachment points, floating gaps, plating, and mutation seams should make the feature feel native to the species instead of pasted on.

**GENERATION ZERO / THE FIRST PAIR**

Noxivitre history starts with two people rather than with a factory model or an ancient naturally occurring population. Riven Solace and Blade were originally Protogens, and neither one began life as a Noxivitre. Their conversion happened when BlackGlass stopped behaving like an outside contaminant or an unusual mutation source and became a permanent body-level rewrite system that their nanites, nerves, synthetic structures, and living tissue all had to organize around. Once that architecture stabilized, calling them mutated Protogens stopped being a complete biological description. The new classification was Noxivitre, the two of them became Generation Zero, and continued maturation eventually placed both founders in the creator-locked Unobtanium rarity.

Riven and Blade are treated as co-origins. There is no canon 'real first one' hidden between them unless future lore deliberately establishes a measurable difference of seconds or minutes, and even then the culture can still remember them as the First Pair. The species exists because the same BlackGlass event reached stable compatibility in both of them and produced a body architecture that could persist instead of burning itself out. Later researchers, communities, or descendants can argue over terminology, but the bible treats both names as equally foundational.

Riven's starting point was already unusual. Before full conversion he was an Uncommon Protogen with traits he normally concealed: his legs floated rather than physically joining the torso in the ordinary way, his tail was also a floating limb, he had horns, and he possessed two biological ear pairs. Those features matter because they predate Noxivitre classification. BlackGlass does not erase that history; it later reinterprets those existing structures through Noxivitre biology, surrounding the floating separations with active nanite bridges, threading the horns with BlackGlass-responsive sensory channels, and integrating both ear pairs into a much denser sensory map. Riven's conversion is therefore the complicated founder case: the body was already atypical before the species-defining rewrite began, and the rewrite grows around those traits instead of pretending they were created from nothing.

Blade gives the setting the opposite comparison. He began as a Common Protogen with a much more ordinary baseline, which makes changes produced by BlackGlass easier to isolate. As his conversion matures, the changes are therefore especially obvious: his once-continuous Protogen facial architecture develops into a mobile Fractureface, new BlackGlass plates begin to float independently around major joints, his tail gains controlled segmentation and temporary decoupling, and his nanites become capable of maintaining anatomy that did not exist anywhere in his original body plan. When broad Noxivitre systems appear in both founders despite their different starting bodies, it is strong evidence that the feature belongs to the new species rather than to Riven's old Uncommon traits.

Both founders eventually developed six full arms: their original pair plus two additional duplicated pairs. The duplicated arms followed each person's own anatomy, fur, plating, proportions, coloration, and mechanical-biological construction. They were not generic blue BlackGlass arms grafted onto the body. As the founders mature further, those six arms stop reading as a single mutation pasted onto a Protogen body and become part of a larger Noxivitre layout: shoulder and rib structures adapt to three independent arm pairs, BlackGlass channels redistribute load between sockets, floating support plates move out of the way automatically, and their body maps treat all six arms as equally native. This became one of the earliest rules of Noxivitre mutation biology: when BlackGlass has a healthy personal template available, it prefers to build from that person's body map. Mutation can be extreme without erasing who the body belongs to.

The founders are important, but they are not mandatory templates. A Noxivitre does not need six arms, floating legs, horns, multiple ears, pink coloration, white fur, a Protogen ancestry, or any exact trait from Riven or Blade. Their role is historical and mechanistic. They prove that BlackGlass can cross the line from mutation into a stable species architecture, they establish the first body-map precedents, and they provide the oldest known examples of nanites learning to maintain anatomy that did not exist in the original body.

Generation Zero status and rarity are conceptually separate, but Riven and Blade are explicitly Unobtanium. They are not Unobtanium merely because they came first, nor because six arms, floating anatomy, horns, or extreme mutations need to be locked away. They qualify because their mature Generation-Zero bodies develop Origin Glasshearts and founder-class body-map authoring: BlackGlass can establish new stable personal anatomy pathways rather than only expressing ordinary inherited or learned mutation patterns. Common through Mythic remain open tiers, and a Mythic character can be visually stranger, larger, or more complicated than either founder without becoming Unobtanium. The lock is tied to creator-approved canon architecture, not to making everyone else's designs less interesting.

### **MATURE GENERATION-ZERO DIVERGENCE**

Riven and Blade keep recognizable pieces of their former Protogen selves, but their mature bodies increasingly stop following Protogen architecture. Their facial displays separate into mobile BlackGlass Fractureface structures rather than one uninterrupted visor shell; plates can float around joints instead of remaining bolted to a fixed armor layout; Vitrite nanites become visible as connective fields during heavy movement; and both bodies acquire structures that exist because the Glassheart actively maintains them rather than because those structures were present in the original genome or chassis.

The change is deliberately gradual. There is no single second where every old feature vanishes and a completely unrelated creature appears. Instead, more and more of the body starts obeying Noxivitre rules until Protogen is useful only as ancestry. The six arms become fully native, facial architecture becomes fracture-based, BlackGlass channels replace ordinary fixed interfaces, and the founders gain personal mutation systems that can create new stable configurations without treating every change as damage.

Later Noxivitres can have several origins. Some may be Converted from compatible pre-existing species, some may be Glassborn with Noxivitre systems from the beginning, some may be deliberately Forged, and sufficiently developed BlackGlass colonies may eventually produce Emergent individuals. None of those origins replace the historical beginning. Riven and Blade remain the point at which the species first became real, stable, named, and repeatable enough that later Noxivitres could be recognized as members of the same biological family.

### **FOUNDATION RULES FROM GENERATION ZERO**

- Riven and Blade are both canonically first; neither is treated as a prototype owned by the other.

- Their former Protogen traits remain part of their personal history and can persist after conversion.

- BlackGlass duplication normally follows the individual’s own body template unless instability or corruption is specifically established.

- Six arms are a founder precedent, not a species requirement and not an Unobtanium-only feature.

- Founder status by itself does not equal rarity, power level, or social rank; Riven and Blade are specifically Unobtanium because their mature Generation-Zero core architecture carries founder-class, creator-locked BlackGlass functions.

- Later origins can expand the species without retconning Generation Zero.

**HOW TO USE THIS BIBLE**

This is meant to be a giant working reference, not a rulebook that makes people ask permission every five minutes. If you are designing a character, start with Part I, Part VI, and Part XI, then jump to whatever anatomy or mutation pages matter to that character.

If you are writing lore, treat the defaults as shared language. Exceptions are allowed when they have a reason. A weird exception is usually more interesting when everyone involved knows what the normal rule was first.

If you and your friends add new canon, write it so it answers at least one useful question: what does it look like, how does it work, what does it change for a character, or why does it matter to the setting. If it answers none of those, it can stay a fun headcanon until it grows into something stronger.

Most importantly, do not let the rarity chart eat the species. The BlackGlass body system, mutable anatomy, nanites, personal identity, and visual construction language are the actual species. Rarity is just a way to organize how extreme a design is.

# **ANATOMY REFERENCE**

A Noxivitre body is best understood as a coordinated stack of living tissue, synthetic structures, BlackGlass, nanites, and a persistent body map. Individual builds can omit, relocate, duplicate, detach, or heavily modify many visible structures without ceasing to be Noxivitres.

| **REGION / SYSTEM**   | **DEFAULT FUNCTION**                                                      | **COMMON VARIATION QUESTIONS**                                                   |
|-----------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Head / face**       | Expression, sensory housing, identity display, protection.                | Continuous vs segmented? Floating plates? Biological mouth? Exposed BlackGlass?  |
| **Glassheart region** | High-level body-map coordination and systemic stabilization.              | Single core, distributed support nodes, unusual placement, founder architecture? |
| **Arms / hands**      | Manipulation and interaction.                                             | 2, 4, 6, 8+? Symmetrical? Floating? Duplicated from original pair?               |
| **Legs / feet**       | Locomotion and posture.                                                   | Plantigrade, digitigrade, floating, segmented, temporarily decouplable?          |
| **Tail systems**      | Balance, communication, locomotion, storage, defense, or pure morphology. | Single/multiple? Floating? Aquatic? Draconic? Mechanical? Shape-changing?        |
| **Back / wings**      | Flight, display, heat management, stabilization, or storage.              | Functional vs decorative? One pair vs several? Organic vs BlackGlass-grown?      |
| **External plates**   | Protection and cyber-organic structure.                                   | Fixed, retractable, orbiting, fractured, translucent, grown only during stress?  |
| **Nanite reservoirs** | Store and distribute Vitrites.                                            | Centralized vs distributed? High-capacity? Specialized local strains?            |

| **GLASSSHIFT CLASS** | **SCOPE**                                                           | **TYPICAL EXAMPLES**                                                     | **MAIN DESIGN QUESTION**                                                |
|----------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Micro**            | Surface or local adjustment.                                        | Markings, claws, plate edges, eye/display changes, small sensory shifts. | Does it reset quickly or become the new default?                        |
| **Minor**            | One recognizable structure changes.                                 | Horn growth, tail alteration, local plating, limited detachment.         | How is coordination learned and stabilized?                             |
| **Major**            | Body plan changes meaningfully.                                     | New arm pair, floating legs, functional wings, torso restructuring.      | What else must move, reroute, or be rebuilt around it?                  |
| **Recursive**        | An existing structure becomes a reusable template.                  | Repeated limb duplication, mirrored plates, repeated sensory structures. | What prevents runaway copying or resource collapse?                     |
| **Systemic**         | Multiple systems revise together.                                   | Whole-body size shift, integrated face/core rewrite, major conversion.   | What preserves memory, identity, and continuity while the body changes? |
| **Founder-authored** | Generation-Zero body-map authoring rather than ordinary expression. | New stable pathways created by Riven/Blade founder architecture.         | Owner/creator approval; do not treat this as a generic Mythic ability.  |

| **STABILITY SIGNAL**            | **WHAT IT USUALLY MEANS**                                        | **STORY / MEDICAL RESPONSE**                                                   |
|---------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Clean synchronization**       | New structure moves naturally and matches the accepted body map. | Normal adaptation, training, clothing/equipment adjustment.                    |
| **Lag / ghost motion**          | Nanite or neural routing is slightly behind conscious intent.    | Rest, recalibration, local diagnostics, coordination practice.                 |
| **Flicker / plate drift**       | Local anchoring or expression geometry is unstable.              | Check nanite reserve, stress load, local BlackGlass integrity.                 |
| **Repeated unwanted reversion** | Body map and current structure disagree.                         | Determine which state the individual actually accepts as baseline.             |
| **Recursive overbuild**         | Duplication continues beyond intended endpoint.                  | Cut triggers, stabilize template boundaries, protect core resources.           |
| **Core desynchronization**      | Local body sections disagree with Glassheart high-level state.   | Medical priority; avoid forcing another major Glassshift until resynchronized. |

### Human Notes & Practical Detail
The best way to understand **Functional Wings** is to imagine the character dealing with it on a boring Tuesday, not only during a transformation scene.

The useful questions here are **load paths, range of motion, proprioception, attachment or suspension logic, and the way nearby structures make room for it**. That is the technical side, but it should show up in ordinary life instead of only in diagrams. With **Functional Wings**, practical details can include folding geometry while sitting, doorway and ceiling clearance, air load during fast movement, and keeping the wings from blocking people in crowded spaces. A character who has had the feature for years usually handles those things without thinking; somebody who acquired it recently may still misjudge space, overcorrect, or consciously plan movements that will eventually become automatic.

Decide what **Functional Wings** feels like from the inside. It might register as ordinary proprioception, pressure, a quiet digital position sense, background diagnostics, or almost nothing until something goes wrong. Useful warning signs for this feature include loss of fine feather or membrane control, one wing locking into a safer folded state, and uneven extension. Those signs do not need to mean catastrophic failure. Noxivitre systems usually degrade in layers, so reduced precision or a safer fallback state often appears before anything truly dangerous happens.

For artists and writers, give **Functional Wings** at least one repeatable visual or behavioral tell. Possibilities include a deliberate half-fold used as a resting posture, support seams becoming visible near the shoulder roots, and nanites reinforcing the leading edge. Keep the tell consistent enough that it feels like the same body from scene to scene. Also test the feature somewhere inconvenient but mundane: a cramped vehicle, an unfamiliar bed, a clinic designed for fixed anatomy, or a crowded hallway. If the design still has a believable answer there—perhaps through harness and clothing design, air load during fast movement, and doorway and ceiling clearance—the rule is doing real work instead of existing only for spectacle.

If a character breaks the normal version of **Functional Wings**, write down what is compensating for the exception and what tradeoff appears somewhere else. The safest fallback is usually a mechanically conservative posture or reduced range, not the anatomy vanishing for no reason. Rarity should still be judged from the whole build. A visually extreme version can remain inside the open tiers, including Mythic, when it does not use creator-reserved founder authority. Unobtanium is not a reward for having the loudest silhouette.

#### Questions worth answering

- Does **Functional Wings** behave differently during a Glassshift, active repair, or emotional stress?
- What does **Functional Wings** feel like when nothing is wrong?
- How does **Functional Wings** affect clothing, furniture, sleep, travel, or personal space?
- What would a Noxivitre medic see about **Functional Wings** that an ordinary observer would miss?

#### Character-facing note

**Functional Wings** can shape habits, accessibility needs, confidence, embarrassment, fashion, or body language, but it should not replace the character's personality. Noxivitres can have extremely different bodies without those bodies deciding morality, intelligence, social importance, or fighting ability. That separation is important to the open-species side of the canon.
