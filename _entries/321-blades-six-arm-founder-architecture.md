Blade’s six arms begin as BlackGlass-driven duplication but mature into a fully integrated Noxivitre torso with three equal arm pairs. His founder lattice favors clean mirrored socket placement and exceptionally precise bilateral coordination.

- Three permanent arm pairs.

- Duplicated arms preserve Blade’s own coloration, fur, plating, and proportions.

- Torso support grows with the limbs instead of leaving them as superficial grafts.

- Neural maps allow independent and synchronized movement.

- No extra pair is treated as disposable just because it formed later.

### **DEEPER CANON**

Blade can synchronize matching hands across different levels with unusual precision. That can be useful for repair, music, crafting, or any task where several actions need to happen in a repeatable sequence. It should not be reduced to combat choreography; six arms are part of his entire daily body language.

As with Riven, permanent six-arm anatomy creates practical posture changes. The torso rotates differently, sleeping positions need more space, and an ordinary chair back can press directly into a lower shoulder socket unless furniture is adapted.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

The Mirror Lattice stores left/right and upper/middle/lower correspondence maps. Vitrites use those maps to detect when one shoulder is loading differently from its counterpart and reinforce the imbalance before it becomes damage.

Mirroring is a stabilizing tool rather than a rule that every motion must be symmetrical. Blade can move six arms independently; the lattice simply has an unusually precise reference for comparison.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Six synchronized arms can overload attention even when the hardware works perfectly. Complex tasks may require Blade to deliberately group limbs into pairs or let some hands rest.

Strength remains constrained by footing, torso stability, and available energy. Six hands gripping something do not automatically make the surrounding floor strong enough to handle the force.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Use six-arm coordination in ordinary scenes, not only action.

- Keep permanent count at six unless @altblade approves a later canon change.

- Let mirrored control be a strength without erasing independent limb habits.
