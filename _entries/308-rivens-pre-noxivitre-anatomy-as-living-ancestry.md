Riven’s concealed floating legs, floating tail, horns, and second ear pair remain important after conversion because they are evidence of what existed before BlackGlass rewrote the rest of him. Mature Noxivitre anatomy incorporates those structures rather than falsely relabeling them as founder mutations.

- Floating legs and tail predate Noxivitre classification.

- Horns predate Noxivitre classification.

- Two biological ear pairs predate Noxivitre classification.

- BlackGlass can modify how those structures function without becoming their original cause.

- Medical records distinguish ancestral anatomy from later authored anatomy.

### **DEEPER CANON**

This distinction matters whenever somebody studies the founders. If a scanner sees a floating tail on Riven, that alone is not evidence that Noxivitre BlackGlass created floating tails. The useful evidence is what changed around the tail: new nanite bridge density, BlackGlass signal routing, dynamic spacing control, repair behavior, and the way the Origin Glassheart now stores the tail as part of a much larger mutable body map.

The same applies to his horns and ears. Mature conversion may add sensory channels, reinforcement, display behavior, or new nanite interfaces, but the original structures are still his. That keeps the lore from swallowing the character's preexisting design every time the species bible gains a new mechanic.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Vitrites mark ancestral structures with stable identity tags rather than treating them as temporary grafts. When repairing a horn, they restore the established horn shape before considering optional newer BlackGlass growth. When rebuilding an ear after damage, they preserve pair identity, orientation, fur pattern, and sensory calibration.

This creates a useful difference between repair and mutation. Repair asks what Riven already recognizes as himself. Mutation asks what new configuration he might choose or tolerate. The two processes can happen together, but the Origin Glassheart keeps the distinction available.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Riven can still choose to alter an ancestral trait later. A horn can change shape, a floating gap can widen, or an ear can gain a new sensor function. The fact that something is old does not freeze it forever; it simply means the bible should remember where it came from.

Concealment also remains a presentation choice. Hiding a second ear pair or minimizing a floating gap does not remove the trait from his body map.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- When adding a new Riven trait, label it ancestral, conversion-era, or later-authored.

- Do not use BlackGlass as a retroactive explanation for every unusual thing he ever had.

- Let old anatomy gain new functions while retaining recognizable continuity.

#### Character-facing note

**Riven’s Pre-Noxivitre Anatomy as Living Ancestry** can shape habits, accessibility needs, confidence, embarrassment, fashion, or body language, but it should not replace the character's personality.
