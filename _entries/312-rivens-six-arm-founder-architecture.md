Riven’s six arms are permanent native anatomy in his mature Noxivitre form. The important founder development is not another arbitrary increase in arm count, but the way his torso, nerves, BlackGlass channels, and posture reorganize until three arm pairs no longer behave like one original pair plus four additions.

- Three full arm pairs share equal body-map legitimacy.

- Each pair has independent shoulder mechanics and neural representation.

- BlackGlass rib and back structures distribute load between sockets.

- Floating plates automatically clear the movement arcs of all six arms.

- Temporary echo structures can appear without changing the permanent six-arm count.

### **DEEPER CANON**

The upper pair remains closest to the old Protogen shoulder position, while the middle and lower pairs occupy founder-grown sockets supported by a widened internal lattice rather than being stacked directly on top of one another. Exact external spacing can be tuned to his design, but the rule is that every arm needs a believable range of motion and room to cross the torso without constantly colliding with another elbow.

Riven gradually stops favoring the original pair for everything. Different hands develop habits: one pair may stabilize objects, another may gesture while he talks, and another may handle fine work. Those habits are learned behavior, not hard-coded job assignments.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Vitrites monitor shoulder stress more aggressively than in a two-arm body because six arms can generate conflicting torque. BlackGlass reinforcement can shift across the ribs and spine in real time, making heavily loaded areas temporarily denser.

When one arm is injured, the core does not automatically redistribute its identity to a different limb. Each arm remains a specific body part, even though the other five can compensate functionally.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Six arms do not make Riven six times stronger. Total body mass, leverage, footing, energy, and structural limits still matter. Trying to pull in six directions at once can destabilize the torso rather than multiplying force cleanly.

Clothing and furniture remain practical problems. Mature Noxivitre garments need articulated side openings or adaptive materials around all three shoulder levels.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Develop personal gestures that specifically use three arm pairs.

- Keep permanent arm count at six unless @n4k2 approves a later canon change.

- Use temporary echo anatomy for experimental extra-limb moments without retconning the stable count.
