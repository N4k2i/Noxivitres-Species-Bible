Blade eventually learns a Noxivitre-specific form of selective decoupling: certain mapped joints can temporarily separate into floating gaps without permanently turning his entire body into Riven-style floating anatomy. This gives him a distinct route into one of the species’ signature mechanics.

- Decoupling occurs only at mapped safe zones.

- Hands, forearms, lower legs, or other owner-approved segments can separate temporarily.

- The body reconnects physically when the state is no longer useful.

- Nanite bridges preserve control and sensation.

- Permanent floating legs are not required for Blade’s baseline mature form.

### **DEEPER CANON**

The distinction from Riven matters. Riven's floating legs and tail are ancestral permanent anatomy that BlackGlass later elaborates. Blade's decoupling is an authored Noxivitre capability layered onto a body that began physically connected. He can therefore demonstrate the same underlying bridge technology without copying Riven's life history.

A common use is clearance. Blade can let a forearm shift around another pair of arms during a cramped repair task, separate a lower leg slightly to adjust footing on uneven terrain, or move a hand through a narrow space while the rest of the arm stays outside.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Before a segment separates, local Vitrites reinforce both sides of the mapped seam and establish signal continuity. BlackGlass then retracts the physical connector in a controlled way rather than tearing it. Reconnection reverses the process and verifies nerve, power, and structural alignment before load is restored.

The Mirror Lattice is especially good at keeping the separated piece oriented correctly relative to its partner.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Decoupled segments have stricter range limits than Riven's permanent floating anatomy because Blade's body still expects physical reconnection as the default. High-force movement usually forces the seam closed.

If a bridge becomes unstable, the segment is recalled toward the attachment point rather than allowed to drift farther away.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Keep decoupling selective so Blade remains visually distinct from Riven.

- Use the mechanic for cramped spaces and fine work as often as for dramatic scenes.

- Define owner-approved safe seams before adding a new decoupling location.
