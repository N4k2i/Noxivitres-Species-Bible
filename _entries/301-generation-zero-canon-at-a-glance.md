Generation Zero is the shared designation for Riven Solace and Blade, the first two stable Noxivitres and the earliest bodies in which BlackGlass, nanites, living tissue, synthetic structures, and a persistent Glassheart architecture became one self-maintaining species system. Both founders are Unobtanium, but the word founder and the word Unobtanium are not synonyms; their rarity is based on what their mature cores can do, while Generation Zero describes historical position.

- Riven Solace — Generation Zero, Unobtanium, owned by @n4k2 on Discord.

- Blade — Generation Zero, Unobtanium, owned by @altblade on Discord.

- Both retain recognizable ancestry from their former Protogen bodies while increasingly following Noxivitre anatomy instead.

- Their shared permanent arm count remains six unless later owner-approved canon changes it.

- Neither founder is the template every Noxivitre must copy.

### **DEEPER CANON**

The most important founder rule is continuity. Riven does not stop being Riven when his face fractures into movable plates, his BlackGlass channels become more visible, or his floating anatomy gains new functions. Blade does not become a generic monster when his body starts segmenting or his nanites gain independent maintenance behavior. Their old bodies are not discarded; BlackGlass keeps reading, copying, and extending them.

A later historian can divide the conversion into medical stages, but the cultural version is simpler: the First Pair crossed the point where their bodies could keep inventing and maintaining Noxivitre anatomy without needing an outside machine to tell them what they were supposed to be. That is the moment the species becomes self-consistent rather than merely contaminated.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Generation-Zero BlackGlass is unusually permissive because it learned its first stable rules inside two different Protogen baselines at the same time. Riven supplied a body already comfortable with unusual floating anatomy; Blade supplied a comparatively conventional body that forced BlackGlass to prove it could construct new systems from scratch. The result is not a merged template. Each founder has an independent body map, but the species-wide mutation language inherits lessons from both.

Vitrite swarms in the founders are older in lineage than every later Noxivitre strain. That does not make every individual nanite ancient; they are constantly replaced. What persists is the instruction lineage, error-correction behavior, and founder-specific interpretation of identity.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

In daily life, founder status is mostly background until somebody makes it weird. Riven and Blade still have to fit through doors, coordinate six arms, maintain clothing, sleep comfortably, and deal with people staring when the BlackGlass starts visibly moving. Their rarity does not force them to act regal, mysterious, violent, or important in every scene.

A Mythic Noxivitre can be physically larger, have more limbs, show more dramatic floating anatomy, or transform more visibly than either founder. The founders remain Unobtanium because of locked core architecture and canon status, not because they must always win a visual-complexity contest.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Keep later founder additions owner-approved and character-specific.

- Do not turn a trait into Unobtanium-only just because Riven or Blade happens to have it.

- When adding a new founder ability, define what body-map mechanism supports it and what prevents it from becoming limitless.

### Human Notes & Practical Detail
For **Generation Zero Canon at a Glance**, the species rules should answer the practical questions first and leave spectacle for when the story actually needs it.

The useful questions here are **Generation-Zero core architecture, founder-only body-map authoring, owner canon, fatigue limits, and the difference between precedent and a public trait list**. That is the technical side, but it should show up in ordinary life instead of only in diagrams. With **Generation Zero Canon at a Glance**, practical details can include sleep, ordinary social interaction, movement, and privacy. A character who has had the feature for years usually handles those things without thinking; somebody who acquired it recently may still misjudge space, overcorrect, or consciously plan movements that will eventually become automatic.

Decide what **Generation Zero Canon at a Glance** feels like from the inside. It might register as ordinary proprioception, pressure, a quiet digital position sense, background diagnostics, or almost nothing until something goes wrong. Useful warning signs for this feature include slower response, temporary fallback behavior, and reduced precision. Those signs do not need to mean catastrophic failure. Noxivitre systems usually degrade in layers, so reduced precision or a safer fallback state often appears before anything truly dangerous happens.

For artists and writers, give **Generation Zero Canon at a Glance** at least one repeatable visual or behavioral tell. Possibilities include subtle BlackGlass seams, visible nanite traffic under load, and a more compact resting state. Keep the tell consistent enough that it feels like the same body from scene to scene. Also test the feature somewhere inconvenient but mundane: a cramped vehicle, an unfamiliar bed, a clinic designed for fixed anatomy, or a crowded hallway. If the design still has a believable answer there—perhaps through maintenance, sleep, and privacy—the rule is doing real work instead of existing only for spectacle.

If a character breaks the normal version of **Generation Zero Canon at a Glance**, write down what is compensating for the exception and what tradeoff appears somewhere else. Founder capability still has costs. Riven and Blade can author pathways that later Noxivitres normally cannot, but fatigue, risk, preference, and owner canon still matter. Rarity should still be judged from the whole build. A visually extreme version can remain inside the open tiers, including Mythic, when it does not use creator-reserved founder authority. Unobtanium is not a reward for having the loudest silhouette.

#### Character-facing note

**Generation Zero Canon at a Glance** can shape habits, accessibility needs, confidence, embarrassment, fashion, or body language, but it should not replace the character's personality. Noxivitres can have extremely different bodies without those bodies deciding morality, intelligence, social importance, or fighting ability. That separation is important to the open-species side of the canon.
