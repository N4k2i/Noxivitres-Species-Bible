The Origin Glassheart is the defining internal structure of founder-class Unobtanium Noxivitres. Riven and Blade each develop one independently as their conversions finish, and neither core is a copy installed from an outside blueprint.

- Origin Glasshearts are self-organizing BlackGlass/nanite/biological cores.

- They maintain identity anchors as well as anatomy maps.

- They can create new stable body-map branches.

- They store mutation history at a depth later cores normally compress or discard.

- Riven and Blade have different internal lattice patterns despite sharing the same core class.

### **DEEPER CANON**

A normal Glassheart asks, in effect, 'How do I keep this Noxivitre coherent?' An Origin Glassheart can also ask, 'What would count as coherent if the body were built differently?' That second question is what makes the founders unusual. It can simulate a new arrangement against circulation, nerve routing, balance, mechanical load, sensory maps, nanite coverage, and personal identity before committing to it.

The core is not a tiny gemstone sitting untouched in the chest. It is a distributed organ with a dense central node and branching BlackGlass channels that reach into major body systems. Damage to the center is serious, but the distributed lattice lets the founders remain alive and themselves through injuries that would otherwise erase too much body-map data at once.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Vitrites constantly compare local anatomy to both the active body map and the Origin Glassheart's archive. When a limb is duplicated, for example, they are not only copying skin and plating. They map the way that person's nerves branch, how the shoulder rotates, where fur changes direction, which scars or markings are considered persistent, and what ranges of motion the founder already treats as natural.

The two founder cores can recognize each other's nanite signatures as compatible without being interchangeable. Riven's core cannot simply take over Blade's body, and Blade's cannot overwrite Riven. Compatibility is used for stabilization and communication between systems, not ownership or remote control.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

An Origin Glassheart can throttle its own capabilities. Most of the time it behaves conservatively because maintaining six arms and a mature Noxivitre body is already a constant computational and metabolic job. Authoring becomes more active when deliberately triggered, during extreme adaptation, or when existing anatomy no longer solves a survival problem.

If the core is overworked, it prioritizes identity, circulation, neural continuity, and existing permanent anatomy before experimental branches. Fancy temporary structures are the first things to dissolve when resources become scarce.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Later Unobtanium cores do not have to be identical to the founder cores.

- Keep the Origin Glassheart distributed enough that one dramatic chest hit is not an automatic off switch.

- Define which changes are active body map, archived branch, temporary scaffold, and rejected branch.

### Human Notes & Practical Detail
**Origin Glasshearts** is one of those details that can look simple on a reference sheet and turn into a dozen small decisions the moment the character starts moving.

The useful questions here are **Generation-Zero core architecture, founder-only body-map authoring, owner canon, fatigue limits, and the difference between precedent and a public trait list**. That is the technical side, but it should show up in ordinary life instead of only in diagrams. With **Origin Glasshearts**, practical details can include filtering overlapping sound sources, sleeping on one side, keeping multiple ear pairs from colliding, and deciding which ears visibly react to emotion. A character who has had the feature for years usually handles those things without thinking; somebody who acquired it recently may still misjudge space, overcorrect, or consciously plan movements that will eventually become automatic.

Decide what **Origin Glasshearts** feels like from the inside. It might register as ordinary proprioception, pressure, a quiet digital position sense, background diagnostics, or almost nothing until something goes wrong. Useful warning signs for this feature include a mechanical ear falling back to basic hearing, incorrect direction finding, and one ear pair muting itself to prevent overload. Those signs do not need to mean catastrophic failure. Noxivitre systems usually degrade in layers, so reduced precision or a safer fallback state often appears before anything truly dangerous happens.

For artists and writers, give **Origin Glasshearts** at least one repeatable visual or behavioral tell. Possibilities include small calibration flickers near mechanical bases, ear pairs turning toward different sounds, and fur or plating shifting around the attachment points. Keep the tell consistent enough that it feels like the same body from scene to scene. Also test the feature somewhere inconvenient but mundane: a cramped vehicle, an unfamiliar bed, a clinic designed for fixed anatomy, or a crowded hallway. If the design still has a believable answer there—perhaps through headphones and headwear, keeping multiple ear pairs from colliding, and sleeping on one side—the rule is doing real work instead of existing only for spectacle.

If a character breaks the normal version of **Origin Glasshearts**, write down what is compensating for the exception and what tradeoff appears somewhere else. Founder capability still has costs. Riven and Blade can author pathways that later Noxivitres normally cannot, but fatigue, risk, preference, and owner canon still matter. Rarity should still be judged from the whole build. A visually extreme version can remain inside the open tiers, including Mythic, when it does not use creator-reserved founder authority. Unobtanium is not a reward for having the loudest silhouette.

#### Questions worth answering

- What misconception about **Origin Glasshearts** would the character be tired of correcting?
- Which parts of **Origin Glasshearts** are automatic, and which can be deliberately controlled?
- What would a Noxivitre medic see about **Origin Glasshearts** that an ordinary observer would miss?
- Does **Origin Glasshearts** behave differently during a Glassshift, active repair, or emotional stress?

#### Character-facing note

**Origin Glasshearts** can shape habits, accessibility needs, confidence, embarrassment, fashion, or body language, but it should not replace the character's personality. Noxivitres can have extremely different bodies without those bodies deciding morality, intelligence, social importance, or fighting ability. That separation is important to the open-species side of the canon.
