Riven’s mature face develops into a true Noxivitre Fractureface. It descends from his old digital facial hardware, but the continuous Protogen-style visor ceases to be the structural rule that holds the face together.

- The face is composed of mobile BlackGlass/display regions with controllable gaps.

- Plate count and exact spacing can shift within a stable personal range.

- Expressions use movement, display graphics, light, and nanite density together.

- The face remains recognizably Riven rather than becoming a generic skull mask.

- Damage can isolate one region without shutting down the entire display.

### **DEEPER CANON**

At rest, the Fractureface can align closely enough to suggest the old visor silhouette from a distance. Up close, seams and depth reveal that the surface is no longer one uninterrupted shell. During strong emotion, speech emphasis, combat focus, sensory overload, or an active Glassshift, the plates separate farther and expose the BlackGlass/nanite medium that keeps the expression coherent across empty space.

This structure is one of the clearest visual statements that Riven is now a Noxivitre rather than an unusual Protogen. The face is not simply a cracked visor cosmetic. The cracks are functional joints, sensory boundaries, ventilation paths, and nanite access routes that can move because the face itself is a mutable cyber-organ.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Vitrites maintain image continuity across plate boundaries. An eye graphic can stretch across two regions, fragment intentionally, or collapse to one plate when another is damaged. Local facial nanites also perform rapid repair because small scratches and impacts would otherwise constantly disrupt expression.

The biological and synthetic layers behind the display do not need to be fully exposed every time the plates move. BlackGlass membranes, furred sections, internal shadow, or dense nanite fields can occupy the gaps depending on the exact design.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Riven cannot casually detach every face plate and send it across a room with perfect function. The farther a primary plate moves from its mapped facial zone, the more signal correction and sensory remapping it needs. Small expressive shifts are cheap; extreme separation is a deliberate state.

A damaged Fractureface may still express emotion imperfectly through the remaining plates, which creates useful visual storytelling without making facial damage equal instant unconsciousness.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Keep the Fractureface functionally different from a cracked Protogen visor.

- Use plate motion as body language, not constant random animation.

- Let Riven sometimes align the plates into a calmer near-visor silhouette when he wants to look less conspicuous.
