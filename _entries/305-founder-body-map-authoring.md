Body-map authoring is the clearest mechanical line between Riven and Blade as mature Unobtanium founders and an open-tier Mythic Noxivitre. It lets the founders create a new stable interpretation of their own anatomy instead of waiting for an existing mutation pathway to surface.

- Authoring applies only to the founder’s own body unless another person explicitly participates in a separate stabilization process.

- New anatomy still has to be physically supportable.

- The process can be deliberate, stress-triggered, or partly subconscious.

- Permanent authored traits require successful integration into nerves, maintenance, and identity anchors.

- Temporary authored traits can dissolve without becoming permanent body-map branches.

### **DEEPER CANON**

The system works through iterative drafts. BlackGlass first produces a low-cost structural concept: a scaffold, a partial plate, a sensory phantom, or a temporary nanite limb. The founder's nervous system tests it. If coordination, balance, sensation, and resource cost are acceptable, the Origin Glassheart allows deeper construction. A successful final pass gives the branch long-term maintenance rules.

This explains how the founders can continue developing visibly more Noxivitre-like features after the initial six-arm mutation. Their first conversion proves the system can duplicate existing anatomy. Mature authoring lets it do things that were never present in the Protogen baseline at all, such as true Fractureface plate behavior, orbital BlackGlass armor, dynamic decoupling zones, and sensory structures built specifically around Noxivitre nanite communication.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Vitrites serve as both builders and test instrumentation. They measure whether a new joint pinches a nerve route, whether a floating segment can maintain signal latency, whether a new face plate blocks a sensory field, or whether an extra structural channel creates heat faster than the body can dump it. The founder experiences some of this as instinct: a new configuration can feel obviously wrong before they could explain the engineering reason.

Because the body map includes personal design language, authoring tends to produce structures that look like Riven or Blade. It does not randomly recolor them into generic BlackGlass blue, erase their original palettes, or force identical founder silhouettes.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

A founder can still make a bad decision. Deliberately authoring too much anatomy at once can create coordination debt, maintenance overload, or a body that is technically stable but annoying to live in. The core can revert an uncommitted branch, but a fully integrated permanent branch is treated as real anatomy and may require another major Glassshift to remove.

The founders also cannot use authoring as an excuse to ignore injury. Building a new limb while the body is hemorrhaging resources into emergency repair is usually blocked unless the new structure directly solves the injury.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Use scaffold stages to make major founder mutations visually readable.

- Allow a founder to reject a technically functional branch because it does not feel like them.

- Keep authoring personal; it should deepen identity, not make every founder form interchangeable.

### Human Notes & Practical Detail
**Founder Body-Map Authoring** is one of those details that can look simple on a reference sheet and turn into a dozen small decisions the moment the character starts moving.

The useful questions here are **Generation-Zero core architecture, founder-only body-map authoring, owner canon, fatigue limits, and the difference between precedent and a public trait list**. That is the technical side, but it should show up in ordinary life instead of only in diagrams. With **Founder Body-Map Authoring**, practical details can include movement, sleep, maintenance, and clothing. A character who has had the feature for years usually handles those things without thinking; somebody who acquired it recently may still misjudge space, overcorrect, or consciously plan movements that will eventually become automatic.

Decide what **Founder Body-Map Authoring** feels like from the inside. It might register as ordinary proprioception, pressure, a quiet digital position sense, background diagnostics, or almost nothing until something goes wrong. Useful warning signs for this feature include extra heat or energy use, temporary fallback behavior, and slower response. Those signs do not need to mean catastrophic failure. Noxivitre systems usually degrade in layers, so reduced precision or a safer fallback state often appears before anything truly dangerous happens.

For artists and writers, give **Founder Body-Map Authoring** at least one repeatable visual or behavioral tell. Possibilities include visible nanite traffic under load, a more compact resting state, and subtle BlackGlass seams. Keep the tell consistent enough that it feels like the same body from scene to scene. Also test the feature somewhere inconvenient but mundane: a cramped vehicle, an unfamiliar bed, a clinic designed for fixed anatomy, or a crowded hallway. If the design still has a believable answer there—perhaps through privacy, ordinary social interaction, and movement—the rule is doing real work instead of existing only for spectacle.

If a character breaks the normal version of **Founder Body-Map Authoring**, write down what is compensating for the exception and what tradeoff appears somewhere else. Founder capability still has costs. Riven and Blade can author pathways that later Noxivitres normally cannot, but fatigue, risk, preference, and owner canon still matter. Rarity should still be judged from the whole build. A visually extreme version can remain inside the open tiers, including Mythic, when it does not use creator-reserved founder authority. Unobtanium is not a reward for having the loudest silhouette.

#### Character-facing note

**Founder Body-Map Authoring** can shape habits, accessibility needs, confidence, embarrassment, fashion, or body language, but it should not replace the character's personality. Noxivitres can have extremely different bodies without those bodies deciding morality, intelligence, social importance, or fighting ability. That separation is important to the open-species side of the canon.
