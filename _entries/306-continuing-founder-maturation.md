Becoming a Noxivitre is not the end of Riven and Blade’s transformation history. Their bodies continue to mature after the species classification becomes stable, and this later period is where they stop looking like Protogens with unusual mutations and start reading immediately as founder-class Noxivitres.

- Maturation is gradual rather than one final instant transformation.

- Old Protogen structures can be retained, repurposed, fragmented, or absorbed into Noxivitre systems.

- Six arms stabilize early compared with later founder-only systems.

- Fracturefaces, orbital plates, origin channels, and advanced decoupling develop later.

- Personal coloration and recognizable character identity remain intact.

### **DEEPER CANON**

The mature body is layered history. A plate can contain hardware from the Protogen era, BlackGlass that grew around it during conversion, and a later nanite-grown joint that allows the plate to float. That is more interesting than pretending every old component was removed and replaced on the same day.

Riven's maturation tends to elaborate structures he already understood—floating separations, multiple sensory points, horns, and complex spatial awareness. Blade's maturation is more visibly constructive because BlackGlass creates systems that had no direct equivalent in his original Common body. The founders therefore arrive at the same species identity through different anatomical routes.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Nanite behavior changes alongside anatomy. Early contamination is reactive and often stress-driven. Mature Vitrite swarms behave more like a normal organ system: they patrol known weak points, pre-position around heavily used joints, clean interfaces before irritation starts, and make tiny structural adjustments without requiring conscious commands.

BlackGlass also becomes less visually chaotic with age. Instead of every adaptation looking like an emergency mutation, plates slide with practiced timing, floating segments hold consistent spacing, and temporary structures assemble along established channels.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Maturation does not require endless escalation. The founders can spend long periods with no major permanent changes because the body has reached a comfortable equilibrium. New features should appear because a story, preference, injury, environment, or owner-approved idea gives the body a reason to explore them.

A later design update can be canonized as maturation rather than a retcon when it preserves established identity and can be explained as a new body-map branch.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Use maturation to integrate future reference-sheet redesigns without erasing earlier forms.

- Let stable periods exist between major changes.

- Give each new feature a practical reason the body would keep maintaining it.

### Human Notes & Practical Detail
**Continuing Founder Maturation** is one of those details that can look simple on a reference sheet and turn into a dozen small decisions the moment the character starts moving.

The useful questions here are **Generation-Zero core architecture, founder-only body-map authoring, owner canon, fatigue limits, and the difference between precedent and a public trait list**. That is the technical side, but it should show up in ordinary life instead of only in diagrams. With **Continuing Founder Maturation**, practical details can include clothing, movement, sleep, and ordinary social interaction. A character who has had the feature for years usually handles those things without thinking; somebody who acquired it recently may still misjudge space, overcorrect, or consciously plan movements that will eventually become automatic.

Decide what **Continuing Founder Maturation** feels like from the inside. It might register as ordinary proprioception, pressure, a quiet digital position sense, background diagnostics, or almost nothing until something goes wrong. Useful warning signs for this feature include slower response, reduced precision, and extra heat or energy use. Those signs do not need to mean catastrophic failure. Noxivitre systems usually degrade in layers, so reduced precision or a safer fallback state often appears before anything truly dangerous happens.

For artists and writers, give **Continuing Founder Maturation** at least one repeatable visual or behavioral tell. Possibilities include visible nanite traffic under load, a more compact resting state, and subtle BlackGlass seams. Keep the tell consistent enough that it feels like the same body from scene to scene. Also test the feature somewhere inconvenient but mundane: a cramped vehicle, an unfamiliar bed, a clinic designed for fixed anatomy, or a crowded hallway. If the design still has a believable answer there—perhaps through sleep, maintenance, and ordinary social interaction—the rule is doing real work instead of existing only for spectacle.

If a character breaks the normal version of **Continuing Founder Maturation**, write down what is compensating for the exception and what tradeoff appears somewhere else. Founder capability still has costs. Riven and Blade can author pathways that later Noxivitres normally cannot, but fatigue, risk, preference, and owner canon still matter. Rarity should still be judged from the whole build. A visually extreme version can remain inside the open tiers, including Mythic, when it does not use creator-reserved founder authority. Unobtanium is not a reward for having the loudest silhouette.

#### Character-facing note

**Continuing Founder Maturation** can shape habits, accessibility needs, confidence, embarrassment, fashion, or body language, but it should not replace the character's personality. Noxivitres can have extremely different bodies without those bodies deciding morality, intelligence, social importance, or fighting ability. That separation is important to the open-species side of the canon.
