Riven’s Unobtanium classification gives him unusual options, not unlimited outcomes. Clear limits keep the founder useful as a character instead of turning every problem into a question of why he did not simply rewrite himself around it.

- Material and energy still matter.

- Coordination still has a ceiling.

- Floating anatomy still has safe spatial ranges.

- Origin authoring cannot ignore identity anchors indefinitely.

- Severe core damage can reduce him to conservative maintenance behavior.

### **DEEPER CANON**

The most important limit is opportunity cost. BlackGlass used to build a new structure is BlackGlass not being used somewhere else. Nanites focused on authoring are not repairing every scratch at maximum speed. Neural attention spent learning an echo limb is attention not available for six permanent hands, four ears, a Fractureface, and floating lower anatomy at the same time.

Riven also has habits. A body could theoretically support a configuration that he personally hates, finds awkward, or refuses to keep. The species system should respect that preference as much as mechanical compatibility.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

During depletion, the Origin Glassheart sheds complexity in layers: echoes dissolve, orbital plates pull into simpler positions, expressive face separation narrows, nonessential sensory channels close, and floating gaps tighten. Permanent limbs and identity-critical systems are protected last.

This produces readable failure states without requiring an instant collapse into a powerless form.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Riven cannot author changes into another person's body by force just because both are Noxivitres. Any cross-body stabilization requires compatible nanites and consent, and even then the other person's Glassheart remains the authority over their body map.

He also cannot use mutation memory to perfectly predict a configuration he has never tested. Simulation helps; lived coordination still matters.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- When a new ability is proposed, name the resource, coordination, or identity cost.

- Use graceful degradation instead of an all-or-nothing power switch.

- Keep bodily autonomy absolute unless a story deliberately centers a violation and treats it as a violation.

### Human Notes & Practical Detail
The best way to understand **Riven’s Founder Limits** is to imagine the character dealing with it on a boring Tuesday, not only during a transformation scene.

The useful questions here are **Generation-Zero core architecture, founder-only body-map authoring, owner canon, fatigue limits, and the difference between precedent and a public trait list**. That is the technical side, but it should show up in ordinary life instead of only in diagrams. With **Riven’s Founder Limits**, practical details can include clothing, maintenance, movement, and sleep. A character who has had the feature for years usually handles those things without thinking; somebody who acquired it recently may still misjudge space, overcorrect, or consciously plan movements that will eventually become automatic.

Decide what **Riven’s Founder Limits** feels like from the inside. It might register as ordinary proprioception, pressure, a quiet digital position sense, background diagnostics, or almost nothing until something goes wrong. Useful warning signs for this feature include slower response, extra heat or energy use, and reduced precision. Those signs do not need to mean catastrophic failure. Noxivitre systems usually degrade in layers, so reduced precision or a safer fallback state often appears before anything truly dangerous happens.

For artists and writers, give **Riven’s Founder Limits** at least one repeatable visual or behavioral tell. Possibilities include a more compact resting state, subtle BlackGlass seams, and visible nanite traffic under load. Keep the tell consistent enough that it feels like the same body from scene to scene. Also test the feature somewhere inconvenient but mundane: a cramped vehicle, an unfamiliar bed, a clinic designed for fixed anatomy, or a crowded hallway. If the design still has a believable answer there—perhaps through maintenance, sleep, and ordinary social interaction—the rule is doing real work instead of existing only for spectacle.

If a character breaks the normal version of **Riven’s Founder Limits**, write down what is compensating for the exception and what tradeoff appears somewhere else. Founder capability still has costs. Riven and Blade can author pathways that later Noxivitres normally cannot, but fatigue, risk, preference, and owner canon still matter. Rarity should still be judged from the whole build. A visually extreme version can remain inside the open tiers, including Mythic, when it does not use creator-reserved founder authority. Unobtanium is not a reward for having the loudest silhouette.

- Does **Riven’s Founder Limits** behave differently during a Glassshift, active repair, or emotional stress?
- What would a Noxivitre medic see about **Riven’s Founder Limits** that an ordinary observer would miss?
- Which parts of **Riven’s Founder Limits** are automatic, and which can be deliberately controlled?

#### Character-facing note

**Riven’s Founder Limits** can shape habits, accessibility needs, confidence, embarrassment, fashion, or body language, but it should not replace the character's personality. Noxivitres can have extremely different bodies without those bodies deciding morality, intelligence, social importance, or fighting ability. That separation is important to the open-species side of the canon.
