The founder section is expandable canon, but changes to Riven and Blade are not free-for-all species edits. Their owners control their characters, while the species creator controls the Unobtanium permission framework and shared NOXIVITRES canon.

- Riven-specific permanent changes require @n4k2 approval.

- Blade-specific permanent changes require @altblade approval.

- Shared species mechanics can be proposed without claiming either character.

- Unobtanium remains creator locked.

- Common through Mythic remain open unless a later public rules revision says otherwise.

### **DEEPER CANON**

This division lets collaboration stay easy. Someone can suggest a new type of Fractureface hinge or a new Mythic trait without touching founder ownership. If that idea is then written specifically into Riven's body, it becomes a Riven change and should be approved by Riven's owner. The same applies to Blade.

A founder owner can also reject a species-wide idea for their character without invalidating the idea for every other Noxivitre. Shared biology provides defaults, not a reason to override character direction.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

BlackGlass canon should remain internally consistent across approved founder additions. If a new ability appears, document whether it comes from the Origin Glassheart, Vitrites, an authored body-map branch, ancestral anatomy, or an external device. That keeps later expansions from collapsing into unexplained powers.

When conflicting drafts exist, the newest explicitly approved founder profile should take precedence, and older wording should be marked outdated rather than silently blended.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Permission systems should stay understandable. Unobtanium is meaningful only if people can tell what is actually locked. Ordinary visual traits should not drift into the locked tier just because a founder uses them.

Ownership credits should remain visible in public founder documentation so people can distinguish species canon from character ownership.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Keep a changelog for major founder-form updates.

- Label speculative ideas as speculative until the relevant owner approves them.

- Whenever a trait is proposed as Unobtanium-only, explain why Mythic cannot support it mechanically before locking it.

# **FOUNDER-CLASS CANON SUMMARY**

Riven Solace and Blade are the first Noxivitres and the first confirmed Unobtanium members of the species. Riven is owned by @n4k2 on Discord; Blade is owned by @altblade on Discord. Their permanent six-arm anatomy remains a shared Generation-Zero precedent, while their mature bodies continue developing true Noxivitre systems including Origin Glasshearts, Fracturefaces, orbital BlackGlass structures, advanced Vitrite behavior, and founder-specific body-map authoring. Riven’s mature systems build outward from his preexisting floating legs, floating tail, horns, and two ear pairs; Blade’s mature systems demonstrate how the same species can construct equally deep Noxivitre anatomy from a former Common Protogen baseline. Their Unobtanium status is tied to creator-approved founder architecture, not to making ordinary extreme anatomy inaccessible to everyone else.
