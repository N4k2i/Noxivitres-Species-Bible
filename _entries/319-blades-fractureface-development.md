Blade’s face evolves from a continuous Protogen-style display structure into a Noxivitre Fractureface composed of coordinated display plates, BlackGlass seams, and nanite-maintained gaps. The transition is one of the most visible signs that his underlying species architecture has changed.

- Resting alignment can still echo his old facial silhouette.

- Active expressions can separate the display into multiple independently moving regions.

- Symmetry is common but not mechanically required.

- Plate gaps serve sensory, cooling, repair, and expressive functions.

- The face remains fully expressive rather than becoming a static mask.

### **DEEPER CANON**

Blade's Fractureface tends to settle into cleaner mirrored arrangements than Riven's, reflecting his core's precision bias. During calm conversation the plates may sit close enough to look almost continuous. During surprise, anger, focus, or an active mutation, the symmetry can open dramatically as cheek, brow, or jaw-adjacent regions slide apart and the nanite medium between them becomes visible.

The face should not be treated as a damaged visor that never healed. It is healed. The separations are the intended mature anatomy, with their own joints, sensory edges, maintenance routes, and body-language vocabulary.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Vitrites can shift display information from one plate to another when a region is obstructed. This lets Blade keep eye and mouth graphics readable while plates are moving rather than requiring every expression to line up with one fixed screen grid.

Local BlackGlass can temporarily bridge a gap with a thin display membrane when environmental protection matters more than mobility.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Extreme plate separation is not free. Wider gaps increase nanite maintenance area and can expose more delicate underlying systems to debris or interference. In harsh environments the face may automatically close into a tighter configuration.

Damage to one plate can distort part of an expression without erasing Blade's ability to communicate.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Keep Blade’s plate motion more precise than random glitch effects.

- Allow the resting face to preserve familiarity with the old design.

- Use temporary membrane closure for environmental adaptation instead of pretending gaps never matter.

### Human Notes & Practical Detail
The short version is useful, but **Blade’s Fractureface Development** becomes a lot easier to write once it is treated as part of somebody’s ordinary life.

With **Blade’s Fractureface Development**, practical details can include impact protection, facial expression, eating and drinking, and cleaning seams between plates. A character who has had the feature for years usually handles those things without thinking; somebody who acquired it recently may still misjudge space, overcorrect, or consciously plan movements that will eventually become automatic.

Decide what **Blade’s Fractureface Development** feels like from the inside. It might register as ordinary proprioception, pressure, a quiet digital position sense, background diagnostics, or almost nothing until something goes wrong. Useful warning signs for this feature include display desynchronization, expression lag, and one plate locking in place. Those signs do not need to mean catastrophic failure. Noxivitre systems usually degrade in layers, so reduced precision or a safer fallback state often appears before anything truly dangerous happens.

For artists and writers, give **Blade’s Fractureface Development** at least one repeatable visual or behavioral tell. Possibilities include a display simplifying when processing load rises, internal light visible through seams, and plates separating slightly during strong emotion. Keep the tell consistent enough that it feels like the same body from scene to scene. Also test the feature somewhere inconvenient but mundane: a cramped vehicle, an unfamiliar bed, a clinic designed for fixed anatomy, or a crowded hallway. If the design still has a believable answer there—perhaps through cleaning seams between plates, facial expression, and eating and drinking—the rule is doing real work instead of existing only for spectacle.

If a character breaks the normal version of **Blade’s Fractureface Development**, write down what is compensating for the exception and what tradeoff appears somewhere else. Founder capability still has costs. Riven and Blade can author pathways that later Noxivitres normally cannot, but fatigue, risk, preference, and owner canon still matter. #### Character-facing note

**Blade’s Fractureface Development** can shape habits, accessibility needs, confidence, embarrassment, fashion, or body language, but it should not replace the character's personality. Noxivitres can have extremely different bodies without those bodies deciding morality, intelligence, social importance, or fighting ability. That separation is important to the open-species side of the canon.
