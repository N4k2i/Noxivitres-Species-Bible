Riven Solace is a Generation-Zero Unobtanium Noxivitre owned by @n4k2 on Discord. His mature form grows out of an already unusual Uncommon Protogen baseline, so his Noxivitre anatomy often looks like BlackGlass learned an existing language of floating limbs and then pushed that language far beyond its original limits.

- Former classification: Uncommon Protogen.

- Current classification: Unobtanium Noxivitre / Generation Zero.

- Permanent arms: six, arranged as three fully integrated pairs.

- Pre-existing concealed traits: floating legs, floating tail, horns, two biological ear pairs.

- Founder system bias: adaptive branching and spatially complex floating anatomy.

### **DEEPER CANON**

Riven's mature silhouette should preserve recognizable pieces of his old design while making it impossible to summarize him as merely a rare Protogen. The biggest shift is structural: his face no longer depends on one continuous visor object, his floating anatomy is maintained by visible Noxivitre nanite fields rather than treated as a simple detached-limb gimmick, and BlackGlass plates can occupy space around him instead of remaining attached to conventional armor sockets.

His body map is unusually good at understanding separation. Because his legs and tail were already functionally detached before BlackGlass became species-defining, the Origin Glassheart has an enormous amount of old sensory data proving that a limb does not need continuous flesh or hardware between endpoints to feel like part of the same body. Later Noxivitre floating anatomy borrows heavily from that precedent.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Riven's Vitrites favor distributed control. Floating legs, tail, facial plates, and orbital structures can each run local stabilization loops while the Glassheart handles the overall spatial map. This means one disrupted bridge does not automatically make every floating component drop at once.

During heavy activity, the invisible-looking gaps can become readable as thin particle streams, dark glass dust, short arcs, or shifting lines of nanite light. The exact visual intensity is state-dependent rather than permanently glowing at maximum brightness.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Riven can hide or visually minimize some unusual traits when the body is calm, but mature Noxivitre anatomy makes perfect Protogen passing increasingly difficult. The Fractureface moves differently, BlackGlass seams react to emotion, and the six-arm torso changes posture even when four arms are folded or held close.

His Unobtanium status does not mean every trait on his reference sheet is restricted. The restricted part is the founder-class Origin architecture and owner-controlled Riven-specific canon.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Keep Riven’s old hidden traits visibly connected to his history.

- Let new Noxivitre traits elaborate separation and spatial control rather than replacing everything with generic spikes.

- Preserve his established coloration and personal design language during every authored mutation.
