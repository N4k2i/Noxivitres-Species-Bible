Blade is a Generation-Zero Unobtanium Noxivitre owned by @altblade on Discord. His mature form is especially important to species lore because he began as a Common Protogen, so the Noxivitre-specific systems that later appear on him cannot be dismissed as preexisting rare anatomy.

- Former classification: Common Protogen.

- Current classification: Unobtanium Noxivitre / Generation Zero.

- Permanent arms: six, arranged as three integrated pairs.

- Founder system bias: precise duplication, mirrored stabilization, and controlled temporary decoupling.

- Mature Noxivitre traits are visibly constructed over time rather than inherited from an Uncommon baseline.

### **DEEPER CANON**

Blade is the cleanest founder proof that Noxivitre anatomy can generate genuinely new structures. His original body did not already come with floating legs, a floating tail, extra biological ears, or Riven's hidden Uncommon traits. When Blade develops a Fractureface, orbital plates, segmentable anatomy, and founder-class decoupling, those systems are clearly products of BlackGlass maturation.

His final design should still look like Blade. Pink coloration, established proportions, familiar markings, and other owner-defined traits persist through the conversion. Noxivitre does not mean repainting him black and blue until the old character disappears.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Blade's Vitrites prefer clean repeatable geometry. Where Riven's systems are comfortable with spatially irregular floating relationships, Blade's founder lattice excels at making duplicated or segmented structures line up with extremely precise mirrored maps. That makes him especially stable when managing three arm pairs and repeated plate structures.

The difference is a bias, not a personality assignment. It describes how the founder core solves anatomy problems, not how Blade has to speak or behave.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Blade's mature anatomy can be less visibly chaotic than Riven's while still being equally Unobtanium. A clean symmetrical design can hide enormous core complexity.

His open-tier-looking traits remain open. Six arms, pink fur, segmented plates, floating structures, and Fractureface designs are not reserved merely because Blade uses them.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Preserve @altblade’s ownership over permanent Blade-specific redesigns.

- Use Blade as the clearest before/after demonstration of species-defining anatomy.

- Keep his founder systems precise and coherent rather than making every mutation intentionally messy.
