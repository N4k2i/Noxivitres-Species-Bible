Rather than copying Riven’s horns and four-ear crown, mature Blade develops a distinctly Noxivitre sensory array built from small floating BlackGlass crown shards and existing head sensors. This gives him a founder-level head silhouette without making the two first Noxivitres look like palette swaps.

- Crown shards float within a mapped region around the head.

- They act as sensors, nanite relays, and orientation references.

- They are not biological horns and do not need to imitate Riven’s horn shape.

- They can fold closer to the head in tight spaces.

- Individual shards can be rebuilt if damaged.

### **DEEPER CANON**

The shards are small enough to read as part of the head rather than a halo weapon. Their positions can shift with attention: pulling forward when Blade is focusing on something close, spreading slightly when he is mapping a wide environment, or settling low when he is relaxed.

Because Blade began without Riven's second ear pair, the crown gives BlackGlass another solution to dense spatial sensing. It proves Noxivitre biology does not need one mandatory head anatomy to reach comparable functional complexity.

### **BLACKGLASS & NANITE INTERACTION**

Each shard carries local Vitrites and a compact sensory surface. Data passes through short-range nanite links to the Fractureface and Origin Glassheart. If one shard is lost, the rest can recalculate coverage instead of producing a total blind spot.

The shards also help orient temporary decoupled body segments by broadcasting founder-specific positional references.

### **RARITY, DAILY LIFE & EDGE CASES**

Crown shards have limited range and cannot scout independently across arbitrary distances. Moving too far from the head region weakens signal quality and eventually triggers a recall response.

Because they are body parts, touching or grabbing them without permission should be treated like grabbing an ear or horn, not like picking up an accessory.

### **EXPANSION HOOKS**

- Let shard arrangement become part of Blade’s emotional body language.

- Keep them visually distinct from Riven’s biological horns.

- Use them as a clean species-specific sensor solution for a former Common baseline.
